## Unproved representation and normal-extension obligations

No surjectivity of restricted WOT compression, inverse embedding continuity,
normal native OU extension, or bicommutant theorem is claimed here. Closedness
of an injective star homomorphism's norm image does not establish WOT image
closedness.

An explicit prefix/tail amplification representation is a possible next route
to the included embedding. Infinite amplification is generally NOT globally
WOT continuous on arbitrary unbounded nets. The correct prospective interface
is continuity for uniformly norm-bounded WOT nets, or genuine ultraweak
normality / preservation of bounded increasing positive suprema. The verified
fixed bounded sandwich and unitary conjugation WOT maps are globally continuous
and are unaffected by this distinction.

Pinned mathlib supplies WOT coefficient calculus and separate multiplication,
but no ready Kaplansky density, bicommutant, or ultraweak/predual normal-map
package was located. Those closure and representation bridges remain explicit
mathematical dependencies before a full normal OU semigroup statement.
