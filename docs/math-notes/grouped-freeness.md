## Intended statement and verified result

Let phi be an algebraic normalized probability space over an arbitrary field,
and let B_i be freely independent unital subalgebras. For ANY function g:I->J,
define C_j as the algebra generated by all elements a in B_i with g(i)=j.
`FreelyIndependent.groupedSubalgebra` (`74c5b33`) proves that the ENTIRE algebras
C_j are freely independent. There are no finiteness, countability, positivity,
trace, norm, topology, completeness, or injectivity assumptions. Fibers may be
empty or infinite.

`groupedSubalgebra_eq_iSup` (`9bce675`) identifies this actual generated algebra
with the join of all B_i in the fiber g(i)=j. Original B_i are included in their
assigned group. Repeated grouping is exactly grouping by the composed function;
taking smaller original algebras makes each generated group smaller.

For a continuous expectation on a topological ring algebra over a Hausdorff
field with continuous scalar action, `groupedSubalgebra_topologicalClosure`
proves freeness of the ACTUAL closed grouped algebras. No completeness, norm,
trace, or positivity premise is added. Original B_i are included in these actual
closed groups.

## Proof dependency chain

1. `6375793`: The existing exact raw-join theorem for consecutive interval
   partitions is essential. For a top product fiber, if the original partition
   preserves macro group labels, both it and the interval partition lie below
   the kernel equivalence relation of those labels. Their RAW Setoid join is top,
   so all final labels coincide. An arbitrary raw join of noncrossing partitions
   need not be noncrossing; no such false assertion is used. This proves mixed
   grouped cumulants vanish for actual consecutive interval products.
2. `6e3b1aa`: Arbitrary finite tuples of words, of DIFFERENT lengths, are packed
   into one ordered tagged list. Its tags give a monotone quotient, surjective
   when every word is nonempty. Ordered extraction of each quotient fiber is
   EXACTLY the corresponding original word. This is proved using sorted finite
   indices and literal list filtering, not an implicit fusion-plan witness.
   The interval-product formula therefore proves mixed grouped cumulants vanish
   for arbitrary word products. Empty words are handled separately by the unit
   cumulant theorem; mixed labels force order at least two.
3. `74c5b33`: A generic multilinear map zero on tuples of generators is zero on
   their coordinatewise linear spans. The proof curries the first coordinate,
   inducts on finite order, then applies ordinary span induction in that first
   coordinate. The empty-order base is explicit. `Algebra.adjoin_eq_span`
   identifies each generated group with the span of its generator monoid.
   `Submonoid.exists_list_of_mem_closure` gives ACTUAL finite words for these
   monoid generators, so the preceding word-product theorem applies. Full
   mixed-cumulant vanishing yields genuine freeness by the already verified
   all-order characterization.
4. `9bce675`: The previously proved topological-closure freeness theorem extends
   this full generated-algebra theorem to actual closed groups, and the join,
   inclusion, monotonicity, and composition interfaces are proved.

A centered reduced-word spanning proof was considered as an alternative but was
not implemented in parallel. The cumulant/interval/ordinary-span route closes
the desired strongest algebraic statement and avoids duplicating normalization
infrastructure.
