# Genuine monotone operator limits

The CFC square root of a positive D proves ||Dx||^2 <= ||D|| re<x,Dx>.
For any normbounded monotone directed net T_j, scalar quadratic coefficients
converge to their true real supremum. Positive increments and the energy
bound control comparable vector orbits; a common upper index gives the
Cauchy criterion for every pair in a tail. The zero norm-bound case is included.

Completeness yields pointwise limits. Uniqueness proves additivity and scalar
linearity, and the uniform bound gives an actual bounded operator S. Strong
closedness of symmetry and nonnegative quadratic forms proves positivity
passes under an eventually-positive hypothesis. Applying this to S-T_i and
B-S proves S is the literal Loewner IsLUB of the net. Individual T_i positivity
is unnecessary for this order conclusion; arbitrary monotone nets may carry
a fixed skew-adjoint part.

Fixed-operator commutation passes through pointwise limits, so the actual
supremum remains in the literal double commutant. No bicommutant closure
equivalence, countability, semilattice index or complete lattice on B(H) is
assumed. This discharges the central operator convergence prerequisite for
normal OU maps. Actual map IsLUB preservation remains the next theorem.
