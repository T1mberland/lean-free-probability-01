## Remaining Goal 10 obligations

This C-star contraction semigroup is not yet an L² or normal von Neumann
extension. L² contractivity requires a proved vacuum/GNS norm inequality.
For a nonspanning orthonormal family, its cyclic L² space is the closure of
`{TΩ | T in tensorFamilyAlgebra e}`, which need not be the entire Fock space.
Do not identify these spaces without a theorem. Normality, strong time
continuity, hypercontractivity, logarithmic Sobolev inequalities, and entropy
monotonicity remain separate mathematical obligations.

Useful next route: construct degree damping on the actual tensor Hilbert sum,
prove `OU(T)Ω = D_t(TΩ)` first on Wick words and then by continuity, and derive
true vacuum L² contraction. Restrict damping to the actual cyclic closure or
extend the induced contraction through the genuine GNS completion. Operator
norm contraction or CP alone must not be reported as a normal extension.
